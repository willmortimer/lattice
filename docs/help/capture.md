# Screen capture

Use **Screen Clip** when you want a screenshot in your workspace — not just on
the clipboard.

## Capture a region

1. Press **⌘⇧2** (macOS) or use the app menu entry for **Screen Clip**.
2. Drag to select the region you want.
3. Lattice saves the capture, copies a PNG to your clipboard, and adds an entry
   under **Capture Inbox** in your workspace.

Open **Files** and look in **Capture Inbox** (or **Inbox**, depending on your
workspace layout) to triage captures into notes, boards, or other folders.

## What to expect

- **Screen Clip** captures a **region** you select. Other capture shortcuts may
  arrive later; use Screen Clip for a partial screenshot today.
- Each capture becomes a normal workspace file you can move, rename, or link
  like any other asset.
- Quick text notes are separate: use **Quick Capture** in the activity rail or
  **⌘/Ctrl+N** for a new page.

Lattice does not use the system Share sheet for this flow — Screen Clip runs
inside the app.
